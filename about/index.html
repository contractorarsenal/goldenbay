About

A San Jose builder, led by its owner.

Gold Bay Builders Inc. builds and remodels homes in San Jose, California.

Building Your Bay Home

Gold Bay Builders Inc. is a residential general contractor owned by David Lerma. We take on whole-home remodels, additions, accessory dwelling units, kitchens, bathrooms and custom homes.

Our approach is practical: define the scope, put it in writing, plan selections and permits before demolition, and keep the homeowner informed as the work proceeds.

How to check a contractor

California requires contractors working on projects of this type to be licensed by the Contractors State License Board. You can check any contractor’s license, bond and workers’ compensation status at cslb.ca.gov. We encourage you to do this for every contractor you consider.
