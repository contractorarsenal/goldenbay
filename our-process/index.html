Process

How a project runs, start to finish.

Six stages, each with a clear purpose. Timelines vary with scope and with city review, which we do not control.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    You describe the project, the property and what you hope to accomplish. We listen first and ask about budget range and timing.

  2. 02

    Site visit and scope

    We look at the property, discuss constraints and define a clear scope of work.

  3. 03

    Written estimate

    You receive a written proposal describing what is and is not included.

  4. 04

    Planning and permits

    Plans, selections and permit submittals are organized so work does not start before it is ready.

  5. 05

    Construction

    Work proceeds in a planned sequence with regular updates and decisions raised before they delay the job.

  6. 06

    Final walkthrough

    We review the finished work with you, address punch-list items and complete required inspections.
