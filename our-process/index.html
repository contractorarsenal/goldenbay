How a residential project usually unfolds.

General guidance, not a commitment. The written agreement for each project sets its timeline, sequence and terms, and city review timing is set by the city.

  1. Step 01

    First conversation

    A first conversation usually covers the project, the property, your goals and a budget range.

  2. Step 02

    Site visit and scope

    Often includes a visit to the property and a discussion of constraints, followed by a written description of the scope.

  3. Step 03

    Written estimate

    A written estimate lets you compare what is and is not included before you decide.

  4. Step 04

    Planning and permits

    Plans, selections and permit submittals usually come before construction starts.

  5. Step 05

    Construction

    Construction follows the agreed scope. Ask how decisions and changes will be handled.

  6. Step 06

    Final review

    Projects usually end with a review of the finished work and any remaining items.
