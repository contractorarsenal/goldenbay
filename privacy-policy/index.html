Information we collect

When you submit an estimate request, we collect the details you provide: project type, location (city, ZIP and optional street address), project stage, budget range, timeline, project description, and your name, phone number, email and preferred contact method.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, prepare an estimate and communicate about your project. We do not sell it.

Analytics

If analytics tools are enabled, they record page views and events such as button clicks. We do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses or project descriptions to analytics.

Service providers

Submissions are delivered by email and may pass through our form-delivery and spam-protection providers.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete information you submitted. California residents may have additional rights under California law.
