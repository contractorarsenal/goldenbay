Common concerns

Renovations go wrong in predictable ways.

  • Unclear scope

    Projects drift when the scope is only discussed, not written down.

  • Budget surprises

    Costs climb when allowances are vague and changes are priced later.

  • Poor communication

    Long silences between visits leave homeowners guessing what happens next.

  • Messy job sites

    Dust and debris spread through a home when the work area is not contained.

Common homeowner concerns, not guarantees. Ask for specifics in writing for your own project.

How it works

How a residential project usually unfolds.

  1. Step 1

    Start with your goals

    Describe what you want to change, the property, and a budget range you are considering.

  2. Step 2

    Define the scope

    A site visit and a written description of what is and is not included help both sides agree on the work.

  3. Step 3

    Compare estimates

    Written estimates make it easier to compare proposals on the same scope before you decide.

  4. Step 4

    Plan and build

    Selections, permits and construction follow the agreed scope. Ask how decisions and changes are handled.

General guidance for homeowners, not a commitment. Timelines, sequence and terms are set for each project in its written agreement.
