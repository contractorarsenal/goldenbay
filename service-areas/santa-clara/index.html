Service area

Building in Santa Clara.

Santa Clara operates its own building division and permit process, separate from San José.

Local considerations

What to know before building in Santa Clara

  • Permit requirements, ADU rules and fees are set by the city, not the county or neighboring jurisdictions.

Permits are issued by City of Santa Clara Building Inspection Division. Check their site for current submittal requirements, fees and review times.

Gold Bay Builders’ service to Santa Clara is being confirmed. Contact us to discuss your project.
