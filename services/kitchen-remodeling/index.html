A kitchen remodel replaces or reorganizes cabinets, counters, appliances, lighting and utilities. Keeping plumbing and gas lines where they are keeps costs down; relocating them adds work.

Overview

What a kitchen remodel involves

A kitchen remodel combines more trades than almost any other room: demolition, framing, plumbing, electrical, ventilation, cabinetry, countertops, tile and paint. Coordinating them in the right order keeps the room usable sooner and prevents rework.

Where walls, plumbing or gas lines stay put, the budget goes further. Where you want to move them, we explain what that adds before you commit.

Common projects

Typical work in this category.

  • Full gut remodel

    New layout, cabinets, counters, lighting and appliances.

  • Layout change

    Relocating the sink, range or an island, or opening the kitchen to adjacent rooms.

  • Cabinet and surface refresh

    Keeping the layout while replacing cabinets, counters and backsplash.

  • Kitchen expansion

    Taking in a pantry, breakfast nook or part of an adjacent room.

Homeowner concerns

What shapes a kitchen project

  • Lead time

    Custom cabinets and some appliances take weeks to arrive. Ordering ahead keeps the schedule honest.

  • Ventilation and electrical

    Range hoods, circuits and lighting often need upgrades that are invisible but required.

  • Storage

    Pull-outs, deep drawers and a defined place for appliances matter more day to day than countertop choice.

Materials and planning

  • Cabinetry: stock, semi-custom and custom options differ in lead time, flexibility and cost.
  • Countertops: quartz, natural stone and other materials differ in maintenance, edge options and fabrication time.
  • Appliances: choose models before cabinets are finalized so openings fit.
  • Lighting: plan task, ambient and accent layers together instead of relying on one overhead fixture.

What helps us give an accurate estimate

  • Photos of the current kitchen
  • Rough dimensions or a floor plan
  • Whether the layout will change
  • Appliances you plan to keep or buy
  • Budget range and target completion

Questions

Kitchen Remodeling: frequently asked

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in San Jose?

Costs vary widely with size, layout changes, cabinetry and appliance choices. A written estimate usually follows a review of the project, and figures are not quoted without seeing the space.

Will I be without a kitchen the whole time?

Usually for a significant part of the project. Many homeowners set up a temporary kitchen elsewhere in the house. We help you plan for it.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel?

Cosmetic updates may not, but moving plumbing, gas or electrical, or changing walls, generally does. Your city’s building department makes the call.

Can you work from my designer’s plans?

Yes. We can build from plans by your designer or architect, or discuss design options with you.
