## Metadata & technical
Title pattern `Page | Golden Bay Builders`; descriptions 140–160 chars; canonical from `PUBLIC_SITE_URL`; `robots` noindex unless `PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING=true`; OG tags. **OG image is an SVG placeholder; social platforms need a 1200×630 PNG/JPG** (pending).

## Structured data
Site-wide: GeneralContractor (name, phone, email, founder, areaServed San Jose, tagline), WebSite, WebPage. Per page: BreadcrumbList; Service (services); FAQPage (services, FAQ, home; no rich-result expectation); Article (guides). **Deliberately omitted:** address, hours, ratings/reviews, license, sameAs, awards.

## GEO / AEO
Direct "Short answer" blocks on guides; comparison tables; explicit entity description; sources cited; server-rendered HTML; FAQs answered concisely; CSLB verification link in About. No guarantee of AI citation.

## Local SEO
San José is the sole confirmed market. City pages carry only verifiable, non-fabricated content (the issuing department, jurisdictional differences). They must gain genuine local material (real projects, local rules) before indexing, or be removed to avoid doorway pages. No NAP address published (none supplied).
